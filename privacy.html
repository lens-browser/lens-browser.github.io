Privacy policy

Lens is a Chrome extension that changes websites the way you describe. To do that, its AI has to see the page you ask about. This page lists what Lens collects, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how to change or delete it.

Updated November 26, 2026

Never sent to the AI or TypeSafe

  • Passwords, typed form values and payment fields.
  • Pages on the banking, payment, tax, benefits and health sites Lens lists, and sites you add to Never send.
  • Your browser cookies.

Lens does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How much of a page the AI sees

You choose this in onboarding and on the Privacy page of the Lens dashboard, for every site or one site at a time. A new install starts at Full page.

Full page
Lens’s AI sees the page’s text and layout and screenshots. Typed form values and payment fields are left out, and so are sign-in pages unless you choose Send this page anyway.
Layout only
Lens’s AI sees the page’s address, its layout and the labels on buttons, tabs and menus. Writing, headings and links become word counts, in the text and in the screenshot.
Off
Nothing from the page is sent, so Lens can’t make new changes there. Saved rules keep running; rules that ask the AI get no answer.

Who receives data

Lens’s server
The Lens service we run. It signs you in, passes requests to the AI and TypeSafe, and stores what this page lists.
Anthropic
Runs the AI (Claude) that writes, repairs and adapts rules, checks Marketplace rules and answers rules that use AI.
TypeSafe (Jev)
Decides whether one of your rules fits the page you’re on.
Supabase
Handles sign-in and hosts Lens’s database.
Our hosting provider
Runs Lens’s server and keeps its log.

Each receives only what’s listed above, for the job named here, under its own privacy policy and its terms with Lens.

Your choices

  • Help improve Lens (Settings, on unless you turn it off): Shares which rules you make, keep or undo and where, not tied to your account or email, and what you typed and the rule code, never text from the pages you visit, so the team can fix failures.
  • Suggest rules on pages that fit (Privacy, on unless you turn it off): Sends a page’s address, title and up to 6,000 characters of its visible text, with your rules’ descriptions and pages you said they fit or didn’t, to TypeSafe (Jev). Full page sites only.
  • Delete my usage data and Delete submitted reports remove what is stored for your account at once. Request details can’t be deleted one person at a time; they expire after 30 days.
  • Rules, connections and everything they collect are deleted from your browser when you delete them on the dashboard or remove the extension.
  • To delete your account, email weblensextension@gmail.com. Data already sent to the AI while you used Lens can’t be called back.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, Lens receives your name and email address and uses them to sign you in, and shows your name on Marketplace rules you publish with it. Lens’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Lens reads other sites you connect, such as Gmail, through your own browser session, not through Google APIs.

Security

Everything Lens sends travels over HTTPS. Your sign-in is stored in the extension, never on a web page, and Lens’s server never receives your browser cookies or passwords.

Changes and contact

When what Lens collects changes, this page changes with it and shows the new date. Questions: weblensextension@gmail.com.
