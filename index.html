What Lens can see

  • Stored in your browser

    Your rules, connections and what they collect live in the Lens extension.

  • Sent only at the level you choose

    When you ask for a change, Lens’s AI sees the page at the level you pick for that site: full page, layout only, off. Passwords and form values are never sent.

  • Always in your control

    Pause a site, turn off a rule, or return to the original page at any time.
